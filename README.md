# NutriTrack — Fat Loss & Macro Tracker

A modern, subscription-free calorie and macronutrient tracker designed specifically for fat loss and progressive weight management.

## 🎯 Your Customized Fat Loss Plan

- **Starting Weight**: 70 kg
- **Phase 1 Target**: 60 kg in 5 months (~150 days)
  - **Pace**: ~0.5 kg / week (safe, sustainable fat loss without muscle loss)
  - **Caloric Deficit**: ~500 kcal / day deficit below maintenance
- **Phase 2 Target**: 54 kg (final lean maintenance goal)

---

## 🥗 Tracked Nutrients & Macro Balance

1. **Calories (kcal)**: Daily budget with live remaining/over indicators.
2. **Protein (g)**: High-protein target (~1.8g/kg) to protect muscle mass during calorie deficit.
3. **Fiber (g)**: Essential 30g+ target to control hunger, stabilize blood sugar, and ensure gut health.
4. **Carbohydrates (g)**: Clean carb energy for daily activity and workouts.
5. **Healthy Fats (g)**: 25% of calories to support hormones and brain health.
6. **Water Intake (ml)**: Quick +250ml glass logger towards a 2.8L - 3.0L daily goal.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **No Subscriptions, 100% Free**: No paywalls, no recurring monthly charges.
- **Works on PC & Mobile Phone**:
  - Open on your computer: `http://localhost:5173/`
  - Open on your smartphone (connected to the same Wi-Fi): `http://172.16.1.109:5173/` (or add to your phone's Home Screen like an app).
- **Hybrid Food Logging**:
  - **Instant Food Library**: Built-in common Indian and international fat-loss staples (Eggs, Oats, Chicken Breast, Paneer, Moong Dal, Roti, Rice, Greek Yogurt, Whey Protein, Chia Seeds, Almonds, Salads).
  - **Live Global Food Search**: Free integration with **Open Food Facts** to search millions of packaged and grocery products worldwide.
  - **Quick Add**: Direct calorie and macro entry in 5 seconds without searching.
  - **Custom Dish Creator**: Save your personal recipes and homemade dishes.
  - **Copy Yesterday's Meals**: 1-click duplicate for repeated daily meals.
- **Weight Loss Roadmap & Chart**:
  - Interactive trajectory chart plotting your target slope (70kg → 60kg → 54kg) against your actual logged weigh-ins.
  - Milestone celebrations when reaching target weights!
- **Zero Data Loss**:
  - Automatically saves directly in browser storage (`localStorage`).
  - **Backup & Restore**: Export full JSON backup file or restore anytime.
